My Journey

From recovery to creative ambition

2020

The Turning Point

Overcame personal challenges and rediscovered my passion for creative work. This period of recovery became the foundation for my ambition to build a meaningful career in design and technology.

2023

BCIT Enrollment

Started the New Media Design & Web Development program at British Columbia Institute of Technology. This decision marked my commitment to bridging artistic vision with technical execution, combining design thinking with development skills.

2024

Freelance Launch

Began taking on design and development projects from local clients and online. Started building a portfolio of real-world work while deepening expertise in user-centered design and responsive web development.

2025–Now

Current Focus

Building this portfolio and strengthening my BCI (Bachelor of Creative Industries) application. Creating design systems and full-stack projects that demonstrate both design thinking and technical execution. Preparing for the next chapter as a creative professional.
